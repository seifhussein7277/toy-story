The Adventure Begins

Story Time!!

🎁
Chapter 01

Andy's Birthday Party

Woody, a traditional pull-string cowboy doll, is Andy's favorite toy. But everything changes on Andy's birthday when he receives Buzz Lightyear - a high-tech space ranger who doesn't realize he's just a toy.

🍕
Chapter 02

Lost At Pizza Planet

Jealousy leads to an argument, causing Woody and Buzz to be accidentally left behind at a gas station. They hitch a ride on a Pizza Planet delivery truck to reunite with Andy.

💣
Chapter 03

Trapped In Sid's Room

Trapped by Sid Phillips. A ruthless neighbor who destroys toys for fun. Woody and Buzz must work together with Sid's terrifying mutant toys to plan a desperate escape before Sid blows Buzz up with a rocket.

🚀
Chapter 04

The Rocket Rescue

With the help of Sid's toys, Woody rescues Buzz! They chase down Andy's moving van, strap on Sid's firework rocket, and literally fly though the air to land right back into Andy's box.

⭐
Chapter 05

Best Friends Forever

Realizing they share the ultimate goal of making Andy happy, Woody and Buzz became unbreakable best friends, ready to face whatever new toys would be brought to Andy.
